PROOF OF THE 2020-21 ROW-MISALIGNMENT REPAIR
source: data/raw/CrimesOnWomenData.csv  (736 rows, 2001-2021)

==============================================================================
TEST 1  ANOMALY DETECTION - is the raw file actually broken?
==============================================================================
Method: for every entity, compare its 2020 total against its 2017-19 mean.
        A ratio outside [0.25x, 4x] is not a real-world movement in
        administrative crime counts; it is a data fault.

        entities tested        : 32
        entities outside bounds: 16  <-- this is the smoking gun

        entity                  2017-19 mean       2020    ratio
        Delhi UT                      11,590          9     0.00x
        Rajasthan                     30,127         42     0.00x
        West Bengal                   26,721         23     0.00x
        Maharashtra                   27,741        158     0.01x
        Chandigarh                       456         18     0.04x
        Uttar Pradesh                 47,741      2,094     0.04x
        Telangana                     14,918        686     0.05x
        Mizoram                          107         22     0.21x
        Odisha                        17,990      3,847     0.21x
        Meghalaya                        299         70     0.23x
        A & N Islands                     62        293     4.70x
        Tamil Nadu                     3,215     15,174     4.72x
        Punjab                         4,554     33,106     7.27x
        Uttarakhand                    2,013     32,338    16.07x
        Tripura                          801     38,523    48.09x
        Sikkim                            55      2,905    52.82x

        Read the two ends of that table together. In a single year Delhi,
        Rajasthan and West Bengal collapse to under 1% of their own
        history, while Sikkim (x52.8) and Tripura (x48.1) explode. Those
        are not policing outcomes; that is volume being re-parked under
        the wrong names. Cases did not move between states; LABELS did.

        Note the filter: entities averaging <50 cases are excluded because
        small denominators make the ratio unstable. D & N Haveli sits just
        under that line yet shows the same signature even more starkly
        (2019 total 7 -> 2020 total 8,861), 
        which is what first drew attention to the problem.

TEST 2  PROFILE MATCHING - which entity does each measure row belong to?
==============================================================================
Method: each entity's 2017-19 mean across the 7 crime types is a
        fingerprint. Match every measure row to its nearest fingerprint
        in log space. The candidate set is ALL entities, so this test is
        free to vindicate the file's own labels.

        2019  (CONTROL - believed clean)
          agrees with the FILE's labels : 34/36
          agrees with NCRB 2020 ordering: 10/34
          -> 2019 vindicates its own labels. The method is sound and
             the misalignment does NOT extend back into 2019.

        2020  (believed broken)
          agrees with the FILE's labels : 10/36
          agrees with NCRB 2020 ordering: 28/34
          -> the measure rows belong to the NCRB ordering, not to the
             labels printed beside them.

        2021  (believed broken)
          agrees with the FILE's labels : 10/36

TEST 3  CONTINUITY - does the repair make the panel behave?
==============================================================================
Method: administrative crime counts are sticky year to year. Measure the
        year-on-year swing distribution for entities with >=200 cases,
        under both readings of the file. The correct labelling is the one
        that produces plausible movements.

        AS-IS (file labels)
          2020: n=28  median|YoY|=  71.2%  mean|YoY|= 282.0%  implausible(>60%)=15
                Jammu & Kashmir +64%, Kerala +85%, Maharashtra -99%, Meghalaya -78%, Odisha -81%, Punjab +538%, Rajasthan -100%, Tamil Nadu +401%, Telangana -96%, Tripura +4313%, Uttar Pradesh -96%, Uttarakhand +1447%, West Bengal -100%, Chandigarh -96%, Delhi UT -100%
          2021: n=27  median|YoY|=  15.7%  mean|YoY|=  15.9%  implausible(>60%)=0

        REPAIRED (NCRB order)
          2020: n=28  median|YoY|=  13.9%  mean|YoY|=  15.2%  implausible(>60%)=0
          2021: n=27  median|YoY|=  15.7%  mean|YoY|=  15.9%  implausible(>60%)=0

        The repair takes 2019->2020 from a median swing of 71.2% with 15
        impossible jumps down to 13.9% with none. No alternative labelling
        was tried and none is needed: NCRB_ORDER_2020 is public record.

TEST 4  INVARIANCE - does the repair invent or destroy any cases?
==============================================================================
Method: a pure re-labelling cannot change any column total. If a total
        moved, the repair would be fabricating data.

        measure       as-is total   repaired total    delta
        Rape              535,702          535,702        0
        K&A               835,023          835,023        0
        DD                158,750          158,750        0
        AoW             1,162,229        1,162,229        0
        AoM               244,884          244,884        0
        DV              1,909,978        1,909,978        0
        WT                 21,156           21,156        0

        All deltas zero. Two consequences worth stating in a review:
          1. The repair is non-destructive - it moves attribution only.
          2. Every NATIONAL figure in this project is identical with or
             without the repair. Only state-level 2020-21 attribution
             was ever at risk, and that is exactly what the repair fixes.

==============================================================================
VERDICT: the 2020 and 2021 measure blocks follow NCRB's post-reorganisation
         entity order (28 states, then 8 UTs with J&K demoted, Ladakh added
         and the two western UTs merged) while the label column was still
         generated from the pre-2019 36-entity list. Repairing the join is
         positionally determined, profile-confirmed, continuity-validated
         and total-preserving.
