Pareto curve — concentration of the burden

Bars are each entity's share of the national total; the line is the running cumulative share. Both are percentages on one axis — no second scale.

Class summary

Concentration ratio is share of burden divided by share of entities. Above 1 means the class carries more than its headcount would suggest.

A = first 70% of cumulative volume · B = next 20% · C = tail 10%. The entity that crosses a boundary is pulled into the class it completes.

Risk zones, 2021

Bands are quartiles of the 2021 intensity distribution, so they stay meaningful if the data is refreshed.

Priority matrix — level against direction

Level alone cannot separate a bad-but-stable entity from a moderate-but-deteriorating one. Those need opposite responses, so both are on the table.

Volume class against intensity band

Where the two segmentations disagree is where the interesting cases sit — especially class C entities in the Critical band, which any volume-only ranking buries.
