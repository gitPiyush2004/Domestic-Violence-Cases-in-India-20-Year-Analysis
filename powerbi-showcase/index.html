Where the two segmentations disagree

ABC bands by volume, risk zones by intensity.

Priority segments

ABC bands by volume; risk zones band by intensity. They are meant to disagree — a Class C entity in the Critical zone is small, badly affected, and invisible to every volume-ranked league table. Thresholds are the quartiles of the 2021 rate distribution (2.5 / 9.1 / 22.9 per lakh women), not round numbers, so they survive a refresh without quietly changing meaning.

The published data is broken for 2020–21. Here is the proof.

As published
Delhi, domestic violence 2019: 3,792
Delhi, domestic violence 2020: 3

Dadra & Nagar Haveli (pop. 344,000), 2020: 2,557

Delhi did not stop having domestic violence, and a union territory of 344,000 did not out-report it.
Root cause
On 31 Oct 2019 J&K became a UT and Ladakh was carved out; on 26 Jan 2020 D&N Haveli merged with Daman & Diu. NCRB's 2020 table lists 28 states then 8 UTs. The CSV pasted that value block against a label column still generated from the pre-2019 36-entity list, where J&K sits at position 9 among the states. Every measure row from position 9 down is attached to the wrong state.

Reporting entities by year

The 34 → 36 step in 2011 is Delhi and Telangana entering.

Three kinds of zero, separated

Conflating them is how a dashboard becomes confidently wrong.

Four independent tests defend the repair
  1. Anomaly bands — 16 of 32 entities fall outside a 0.25×–4× band against their 2017–19 mean.
  2. Crime-mix profile matching in log space — a 2019 control has 34/36 entities matching their own labels; 2020 has only 10/36, but 28/34 match NCRB's published order.
  3. Year-over-year continuity — median absolute swing falls from 71.2% to 13.9%; implausible jumps above 60% fall from 15 to 0.
  4. Invariance — all seven measure totals are unchanged. The repair re-labels; it never invents.

The 2019 control is the one that matters: running the same test on a year believed correct and getting 34/36 proves the method detects misalignment rather than manufacturing it. Because the repair only moves attribution, every national figure in this report is identical with or without it.

Source: NCRB Crime in India 2001–2021 (Kaggle mirror), denominators from Census 2011. NCRB counts cases reported to police, not incidents — every figure here is a reporting measure.
